Long-running selections the market should price generously. Higher odds mean lower probability — treat these as value, not bankers.
Three independent tickets built from every sport's streak model. A ticket takes the strongest legs available, so it may mix sports — or end up all football, all basketball, whatever the runs say that day. Every ticket carries at least 15 legs, the minimum SportyBet's Flexi option wants, with max 5 losses as the insurance line; no team appears twice on one ticket and the day's matches are shared out between the three tickets, so they do not cannibalise each other (a second leg from the same match is marked in the table when it is needed to reach 15). Every leg kicks off inside the next 24 hours: the day's stake settles and rolls into tomorrow, which is what makes the compounding work. Every leg is priced at 1.50 or better. Only place a leg if SportyBet's price is at or above its break-even — that is what keeps the ticket value-positive. The Flexi figures are arithmetic on the modelled prices, not SportyBet's internal split, so check their calculator before staking.
